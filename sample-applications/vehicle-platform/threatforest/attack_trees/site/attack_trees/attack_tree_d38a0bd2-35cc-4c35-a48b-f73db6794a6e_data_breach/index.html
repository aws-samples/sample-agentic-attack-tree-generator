    Attack Tree: Data Breach

    Threat ID: d38a0bd2-35cc-4c35-a48b-f73db6794a6e Statement: A threat actor with access to an IAM Principal with modify access to the DynamoDB tables can delete the data, resulting in reduced availability of vehicle registration, vehicle listing, and registration status.
